## Selected Artworks

A collection of my paintings exploring abstract forms, color relationships, and emotional expression. These works span from 2021 to the present, showcasing my evolving artistic practice through various series and experiments.

Media: mixed media (acrylic, spraypaint, marker pen) on canvas.
